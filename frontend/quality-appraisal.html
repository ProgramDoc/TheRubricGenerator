Each selected paper is appraised with the validated tool matching its study classification.

  • Classify study design (using OGAI Annotator)
  • Extract structured fields (universal + type-specific + design modifiers)
  • Auto-pick the paper's primary outcome

  • Run the reporting-guideline checklist (v1: CONSORT 2025 for RCTs)
